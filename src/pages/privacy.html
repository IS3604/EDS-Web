Introduction

EditSimple Editing Services Private Limited ("EditSimple", "we", "our" or "us") provides YouTube and social video services to medical practices. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information about visitors to our website, practices that contact us, and the practices we work with.

1. Patient Information and HIPAA

We will never disclose, publish, sell or share any patient information.

  • Our services are designed so that we do not need, request or receive Protected Health Information (PHI), as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA).
  • The content we produce is educational. No patient names, faces, images, records, case details or other identifying information appear in any script or video we create.
  • We ask every practice not to send us PHI and not to include patients or patient information in recordings.
  • If PHI ever reaches us by mistake, we will not use or share it, we will notify the practice, and we will securely delete it.
  • We will sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) on request before any work begins. Where a BAA is in place, it governs how any PHI is handled, in line with HIPAA.
  • We never enter patient information into AI tools, scheduling platforms or any other third-party service.
  • The only exception to non-disclosure is where we are legally compelled to disclose information, such as by a court order. In that case we will disclose only what is strictly required and, where the law allows, notify the practice first.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, practice name and specialty, and anything you write in our forms or emails.
  • Booking details: information you provide when you schedule a call with us.
  • Client materials: recordings, scripts, logos, brand guidelines and other files you share for your videos.
  • Account connections: manager access to your YouTube channel and connections to other platforms through our scheduling platform. We never collect your passwords.
  • Billing details: invoicing information. Card or bank details are handled by our payment providers, not stored by us.
  • Technical data: basic information such as IP address and browser type, which is processed by our website host and font provider when you visit our site.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule calls
  • To plan, script, edit and publish your videos
  • To report on your channel's performance
  • To send invoices and service updates
  • To improve our services

We only send marketing emails to people who have contacted us or agreed to receive them, and every such email includes a way to opt out.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with:

  • Members of our team who work on your account, all of whom are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as cloud file storage, video review, social media scheduling, email delivery, call scheduling and payment processing, strictly to deliver our services
  • Authorities, only where we are legally required to

Patient information is never shared with anyone, as described in Section 1.

5. Social Media Account Access

  • For YouTube, you add EditSimple as a manager so we can upload and manage videos for you.
  • For other platforms, you connect your accounts once through a secure link from our third-party scheduling platform, which we use to schedule and publish posts.
  • We only use this access to provide our services. You can remove it at any time, and we will ask you to remove it when our services end.

6. Use of AI Tools

We may use AI tools to help with research, drafting and editing. We never enter patient information into AI tools. You can ask us not to use AI tools for your account.

7. Data Security

  • Files are kept in access-controlled cloud storage, shared only with the team members working on your account.
  • Access is reviewed and removed when someone no longer needs it.
  • Account access uses platform permissions and secure connections, never shared passwords.
  • Regular backups protect your materials against loss.

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will notify affected clients promptly if we become aware of a security incident.

8. Data Retention

  • We keep client materials for up to 12 months after our services end, then securely delete them, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or keep them longer.
  • Enquiry details from practices that don't become clients are kept for up to 24 months, or deleted sooner on request.
  • Any PHI received by mistake is deleted as soon as it is identified.
  • We may keep invoices and records for longer where the law requires it.

9. International Data Transfers

Our team is based in India, and some of our service providers are based in the United States and other countries. Your information may be processed in these countries, always with the protections described in this policy.

10. Cookies and Website Data

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. If you book a call, you will be taken to our scheduling provider, which has its own privacy policy.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under US state privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Opt out of marketing emails

We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. To make a request, email us at contact@editsimple.in. We will respond within 30 days.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are for medical practices and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date and notify active clients of significant changes by email.
