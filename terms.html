Introduction

If you sign a separate service agreement, proposal or Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with us, that document takes priority over these Terms wherever the two differ.

1. Our Services

EditSimple provides done-for-you YouTube and social video services for medical practices, which may include:

  • Content strategy and monthly content planning
  • Long-form and short-form script writing
  • Recording guidance (equipment, setup and shot lists)
  • Video editing, captions and graphics
  • Thumbnail design and title writing
  • Uploading, optimization and scheduling on YouTube and other social platforms
  • Monthly performance reporting

The exact deliverables, quantities and price for your practice are set out in your written proposal or service agreement.

2. Free Founding Offer

  • Eligible practices may receive one edited long-form video and five shorts free of charge, based on one recording session.
  • No payment card or contract is required to take part.
  • The offer is intended for practices that are in a position to continue on a paid monthly package if they are happy with the work. There is no obligation to continue.
  • If you choose not to continue, we ask for a short written or video testimonial about your experience. You may decline, and you will always see and approve any testimonial before we use it.
  • You keep the delivered videos either way.
  • We may limit or end the founding offer at any time, and we decide which practices are eligible.

3. Patient Privacy and HIPAA

We will never disclose, publish, sell or share any patient information.

  • Our content is educational. Scripts and videos do not include patient names, faces, images, records, case details or any other information that could identify a patient.
  • Our services are designed so that we do not need, request or receive Protected Health Information (PHI), as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA).
  • You agree not to send us PHI, and not to include patients or patient information in recordings, unless we have first signed a BAA that covers it.
  • If PHI ever reaches us by mistake, we will not use or share it, we will notify you, and we will securely delete it.
  • We will sign a Business Associate Agreement on request before any work begins. Where a BAA is in place, it governs how any PHI is handled.
  • The only exception to non-disclosure is where we are legally compelled to disclose information, such as by a court order. In that case we will disclose only what is strictly required and, where the law allows, notify you first.

4. Your Responsibilities

  • Record your scripts on schedule so we can deliver on time.
  • Make sure all medical statements you record are accurate and appropriate. You are the licensed professional, and the clinical content of each video is your responsibility.
  • Follow the rules that apply to your practice, including state medical board advertising rules and FTC guidelines.
  • Only provide materials (logos, music, images, footage) that you have the right to use.
  • Keep patient information out of all recordings and materials, as described in Section 3.

5. Approval and Publishing

  • We send each month's scripts to you in one batch before your recording session.
  • You read every script on camera and may change any line while recording. Recording a script counts as your approval of its content.
  • Once recorded, we edit and publish videos on the agreed schedule without needing further approval, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing.
  • You may ask us to edit or remove any published video at any time, and we will do so as soon as reasonably possible.

6. Account Access

  • Your YouTube channel and social media accounts always remain owned by you.
  • For YouTube, you add EditSimple as a manager so we can upload and manage videos on your behalf.
  • For other platforms, you connect your accounts once through a secure link from our third-party scheduling platform, which we then use to schedule and publish posts.
  • We never ask for your account passwords.
  • You can remove our access at any time. When our services end, we will stop using your accounts and ask you to remove our access.

7. Fees and Payment

  • Fees, currency and billing dates are set out in your proposal or service agreement.
  • Monthly packages are billed in advance at the start of each billing month.
  • We do not charge setup or onboarding fees.
  • If an invoice is unpaid after its due date, we may pause work until it is paid.

8. Term, Notice and Cancellation

The invoice for that following month remains payable, and we keep delivering the agreed work until the service ends. For example, if your service started on November 1 and you give notice on November 24, the December 1 invoice is payable and your service ends on December 31.

  • Fees for a billing month that has already started are non-refundable.
  • EditSimple may also end the service with one month's written notice, or immediately if you seriously breach these Terms (for example, by repeatedly sending patient information or not paying).
