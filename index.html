STILL GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much of my time does this take?

+

Usually about two hours a month for one recording session. Everything before and after it is handled by our team.

Do I have to approve every video before it's posted?

+

No. Our scripts are researched and checked before they reach you, and you've seen every word by the time you've recorded it. From there, we edit and publish on schedule without waiting on you.

I'm not comfortable on camera. Will this still work?

+

Most doctors aren't at first. Our scripts are written the way you actually talk to patients, you read from a teleprompter, and editing takes care of the rest. It gets easier after the first session.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

+

Yes. Our content is educational and never includes patient information. We handle files securely, and we'll sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) on request.

Will local patients actually find me?

+

We plan topics around what people in your area search for and optimize titles and descriptions for your city and services. Your videos also become content you can use on your website, Google profile and social pages.

What equipment do I need?

+

We highly recommend a proper setup: a good camera, a microphone, lighting and a teleprompter. At the bare minimum you'll need a teleprompter, a tripod to keep your phone steady and a couple of proper lights. For a fully professional look you can add a green screen, but that needs a proper lighting setup to work well. We'll help you choose what fits your space and budget.

When will I see results?

+

YouTube rewards consistency. Most channels need a few months of regular publishing before they gain real traction, and growth builds as your library grows. Shorts usually start reaching people sooner. We report every month so you can see exactly what's working.

We already have a marketing agency. Can we still work together?

+

Yes. We focus on video production and channel management, so we fit alongside your existing website, SEO or ads agency without stepping on anyone's toes.

Who owns the channel?

+

You do. Your channel and social accounts stay in your practice's name. On YouTube, you add us as a manager so we can upload on your behalf. For the other platforms, you log in once through a secure link from our scheduling tool, and we handle posting from there. We never need your passwords, and you can remove our access anytime.

How much does it cost?

+

It depends on how many videos you want each month. We'll go through packages and pricing on the call. You get one clear monthly price, with no setup fees or hidden charges.
